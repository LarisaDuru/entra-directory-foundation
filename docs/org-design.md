# Organization design


## Group Design: Documents why each group exists, including SEC-Dept-Executive, SEC-Dept-IT, SEC-Dept-Finance, SEC-Dept-Sales, SEC-Dept-HR, SEC-Dept-Contractors, and SEC-Role-Helpdesk.
## Department Structure:Department groups provide a simple way to organize users and manage access for a small organization. The design also recognizes that department alone is not enough to describe every user's responsibility.
## Role-Based Access: SEC-Role-Helpdesk was created separately because Help Desk is a job function rather than a department. A user can therefore belong to both SEC-Dept-IT and SEC-Role-Helpdesk.
## Contractors: Contractors are placed in their own group because they are external workers with fixed contract dates. Their access should be reviewed and removed when their contracts end.
## Scaling: At 500 users, the organization would need more role-based groups, automated membership, stronger joiner/mover/leaver processes, and regular access reviews.
## Corrections: Inconsistent department values and group names were identified and corrected. This demonstrated the importance of establishing standards before building the directory.
